# Sungrove Foods credit policy (revision 6, effective 2026-01-15)

- Credit limit for Nusa Retail Group: 60000.00 USD.
- Recommend a credit hold when past-due receivables exceed 25% of the credit limit after all identified receipts are applied.
- Credit holds are approved only by Bambang Wijaya; analysts recommend, they do not apply holds.
- Receipts are applied only to the invoices named on the customer's remittance advice.
